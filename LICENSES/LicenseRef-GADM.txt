GADM licence

Source: https://gadm.org/license.html (retrieved 2026-09-30). Quoted verbatim:

  The data are freely available for academic use and other non-commercial use.
  Redistribution or commercial use is not allowed without prior permission.

  Using the data to create maps for publishing of academic research articles is allowed.
  Thus you can use the maps you made with GADM data for figures in articles published by
  PLoS, Springer Nature, Elsevier, MDPI, etc. You are allowed (but not required) to
  publish these articles (and the maps they contain) under an open license such as CC-BY
  as is the case with PLoS journals and may be the case with other open access articles.

The same page lists one country whose data carries a different licence (Austria). India's
data is under the terms above.

In this repository these terms apply to the district boundaries and to the files derived
from them (see REUSE.toml). They are used here for a non-commercial Smart India Hackathon
prototype; see docs/boundary-geometry-licensing.md. Any other use, redistribution
included, needs GADM's prior permission.
