# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Sanket to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever
their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant]. The summary below
is how it applies here; the Covenant's full text is the authority.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Explain, do not belittle.
- Disagree with evidence. In this project that means data, measurements and runs at real
  scale, never who is more senior or more certain.
- Give and take criticism of work gracefully, and own your mistakes. Many of this
  project's most useful documents record things that went wrong.
- Focus on what is best for the people who rely on forecasts.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and
when someone represents the project in public.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers through GitHub (**@Bhushan2318**). Reports are
handled promptly and in confidence. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions
that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is
harmful, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines][enforcement].
